I build systems that make AI inference efficient. My research asks a simple question: how do we get the most out of the GPUs we already have? I work on hardware-aware routing, scheduling, and orchestration for large-scale LLM serving — raising cluster utilization and cutting tail latency without giving up accuracy or SLOs.

My recent work includes HW-Router (DAC 2026), a hardware-aware router for scalable multi-LLM serving, and InfraMind (arXiv 2026), an infrastructure-aware multi-agent orchestration framework.

I am looking for a research internship (Summer 2027) in ML systems and efficient inference. Get in touch.

Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at Kinetik, a US healthcare startup, and an active competitive programmer — I represented my university at the ICPC Asia West regionals.
